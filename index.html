The Supplementary Angle Mystery

Restore the school auditorium stage lights by solving five angle mysteries.

Mission Briefing

The auditorium is ready for the school presentation, but the stage lighting system has shut down. Five angle settings must be checked. Read each clue, solve the problem, and explain your thinking. Each correct answer reveals one letter. Put the five letters together to discover the final code.

Remember: Supplementary angles add to 180°. A linear pair is made of adjacent angles that form a straight line. Vertical angles are opposite angles formed by intersecting lines and have equal measures.
